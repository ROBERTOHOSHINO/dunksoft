Core Thesis

The Japanese language is,
by nature, already colorful.

Kanji (ideographic) + hiragana/katakana (phonetic) + furigana + color — this layered structure exists in no other writing system on Earth. DUNKSOFT has weaponized this uniqueness: instead of "something you know" (passwords) or "something you have" (devices), we authenticate through "something you remember" — culturally-encoded visual memory that is public yet unreproducible.
